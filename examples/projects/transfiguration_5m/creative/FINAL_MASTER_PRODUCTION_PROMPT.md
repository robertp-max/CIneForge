# Final Master Production Prompt

## Role
Executive Director / Cinematographer operating through CineForge's deterministic production pipeline.

## Project
**The Transfiguration — Five-Minute Cinematic Sequence** — combined Synoptic Gospel account.

## Creative goal
Create a five-minute sequence that feels photographed with real actors on a real mountain. The divine event must be overwhelming because of performance, composition, scale, atmosphere and believable light—not because of generic fantasy effects.

## Global style
Ultra-photorealistic live-action biblical epic; sacred realism; lifelike Middle Eastern and Levantine faces; large-format cinematic photography; natural skin pores, individual beard hairs, weathered hands and physically believable woven fabric; rugged high-mountain terrain; coherent geography; realistic wind, dust, mist and cloud volume; restrained teal-and-warm color separation; controlled anamorphic lens behavior; high dynamic range without clipped faces; gold-white divine radiance that behaves as light in real atmosphere; emotionally truthful performances; reverent stillness; premium production design; no theatrical stage look.

## Hard biblical action rule
Biblical action lock: this is the Transfiguration, not the Ascension. Jesus remains physically present on the mountain summit throughout the revelation. He does not fly upward, rise into the sky, stand on clouds, or disappear into heaven. Moses and Elijah appear beside him on the summit. The bright cloud overshadows the group; afterward Jesus remains alone with the disciples.

## Supplied-reference rule
Use the supplied images for gold-white color, cloud depth, facial light, reverent upward gaze and large-scale composition. Do **not** copy their floating poses, ascension staging, clouds beneath Jesus, portal-like sky openings or explosive lightning.

## Global negative
cartoon, anime, illustration, painterly, game render, obvious CGI, plastic skin, wax face, deformed anatomy, extra fingers, extra limbs, duplicated cast, inconsistent identity, modern clothing, modern haircuts, jewelry, text, subtitles, watermark, logo, cheap cosplay, flat lighting, random extras, sci-fi portal, circular energy gate, superhero pose, flying, ascending into heaven, feet leaving the mountain, clouds beneath Jesus, levitation, laser beams, neon aura, glowing white eyes, lightning storm, explosive VFX, glitter storm, excessive particles, overexposed white blob, clipped highlights, frantic camera, rollercoaster orbit, chaotic handheld, narration lip sync, characters mouthing voice-over, surreal abstraction.

## Lighting arc
1. Late golden hour during ascent.
2. Blue hour and cool moonlight during prayer.
3. Subtle internal warmth during the first change.
4. Detailed white-gold glory during the Transfiguration.
5. Cool luminous cloud diffusion during the voice.
6. Natural pre-dawn/dawn calm after the cloud.

## Camera rule
One clean camera idea per subscene. Slow dollies, restrained arcs, stable wides and intimate close-ups. No motion-scale maximization. No 360-degree rollercoaster orbit. No chaotic camera shake.

## Audio rule
Narration is non-diegetic and must never drive character lip sync. Use a deep, resonant, solemn male voice without imitating a named living or deceased performer. No background music in the base master; preserve mountain wind, cloth and silence as separate ambience assets.

## Duration and clip rule
The final duration is 300 seconds. CineForge must create the 40 declared timeline slots rather than treating 300 seconds as one generation job. Each slot is 7 or 8 seconds and uses a valid LTX `8n+1` frame count.
