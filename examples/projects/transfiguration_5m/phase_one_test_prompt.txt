Create a five-minute sacred cinematic narrative of the Transfiguration using the combined Synoptic Gospel account.

Jesus leads Peter, James, and John up a high mountain to pray after a physically demanding ascent.
The disciples become exhausted and struggle with sleep while Jesus prays.
Jesus is transfigured before them, and his face and clothing become filled with detailed white-gold glory.
Moses and Elijah appear beside Jesus and speak with him on the mountain summit.
Peter, overwhelmed and uncertain, proposes making three shelters or tents.
A bright cloud overshadows and envelops the group.
The Father's voice comes from the cloud, identifies the beloved Son, and commands the disciples to listen to him.
Peter, James, and John fall to the ground in fear.
Jesus touches the disciples, reassures them, and tells them not to be afraid.
When they look up, Moses and Elijah are gone; Jesus remains alone with them on the mountain, and the revelation settles into a quiet aftermath before their descent.

Visual style: ultra-photorealistic live-action sacred realism, lifelike Levantine faces, rugged mountain geography, physically believable woven fabric, restrained large-format camera language, natural wind and mist, and gold-white radiance that behaves as real atmospheric light.
Tone: reverent, emotionally truthful, overwhelming at the climax, compassionate and quiet in the resolution.
Hard rule: this is the Transfiguration, not the Ascension. Jesus remains physically present on the mountain. Jesus does not fly upward, ascend into heaven, stand on clouds, or disappear into the sky.
Use non-diegetic narration with intentional silence and no narration lip sync. Do not add events that contradict the supplied Gospel sequence.
